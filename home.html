About Us

Driving Mining Sector Transformation

Lopex Pacific is a Papua New Guinea-based consultancy focused on mining sector reform, technical appraisal, and governance. We are formally registered with the Investment Promotion Authority (IPA) and maintain full compliance with the Internal Revenue Commission (IRC).

Headquartered in Port Moresby with active project offices in Honiara, Solomon Islands, we operate at the forefront of mining sector transformation across the Pacific, with a growing footprint in Africa and global advisory networks.

Regional Expertise

Deep familiarity with Pacific legal systems, governance structures, and cultural contexts.

Technical Integration

Frontline deployment of FlexiCadastre, GIS mapping, and lab data integration.

Stakeholder-Centric

Participatory governance ensuring reforms are inclusive, equitable, and sustainable.

Strategic Communication

Translating complex technical and legal concepts into accessible formats for diverse audiences.

Sam Kaupa

Principal Consultant
Chief Mining Sector Advisor – Solomon Islands Government

BE (Mining) | MAusIMM | MIEPNG

Sam is a mining engineer and strategic advisor with over 30 years of experience. He currently advises the Solomon Islands Prime Minister on resource sovereignty, fiscal security, and long-term mineral governance. Previously, he served as the Chief Mining Sector Advisor for the World Bank's SMDTAP project.

Before his advisory roles, Sam held senior leadership positions including Deputy Manager at PNG's Mineral Resources Authority (MRA), and Chief Mining Engineer or Mine Superintendent for major international operators like Barrick Gold, BHP Billiton, AngloGold Ashanti, and Perseus Mining across PNG, Africa, and the Pacific.
